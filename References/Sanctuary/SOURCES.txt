SANCTUARY REFERENCE SOURCES
Downloaded for local architectural study, 2026-10-18. Not runtime assets.

Sainte-Chapelle, Centre des monuments nationaux, official visitor guide:
https://www.sainte-chapelle.fr/en/content/download/9827901/file/Document%20de%20visite%20Sainte%20Chapelle%20en.pdf?inLanguage=eng-GB&version=37
Local: Sainte-Chapelle-visitor-guide.pdf
Examined upper-chapel plan on page 2: axial nave, repeated bays, terminal apse.
Applied: coherent bay spacing and a glass sanctuary at the end of the aisle.
This design is a stylized open sanctuary, not a reconstruction.

V&A, The Gothic style - an introduction:
https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-gothic-style-an-introduction
Local: Westminster-buttress-study.jpg
https://vanda-production-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/2019/06/17/10/41/02/f8797750-8615-42dc-87f6-21fe6205a856/buttress.jpg
Examined the supporting pier, upper connection and buttress slope.
Applied: buttresses placed on the same axes as the transverse arches; supports
connect to actual piers, rather than acting as detached decorative pieces.

Minecraft, Kiel Cathedral, builder kimandjax, article by Kate Gray:
https://www.minecraft.net/fr-ca/article/kiel-cathedral
Local: Kiel-cathedral-construction.jpeg
https://www.minecraft.net/content/dam/minecraftnet/archive/ProgressONE.jpeg
Examined the construction cutaway, tall repeated openings and layered depth.
Applied: an exposed interior that communicates scale without enclosing the view.
The build is original geometry; no schematic was imported.
