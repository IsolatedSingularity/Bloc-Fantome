# Current roadmap

Owner direction, 24 October 2026. This is the single current roadmap. Dated
handoffs and research remain historical evidence, not additional authorization.

## Preserved 3.0 rollback

3.0.0, `46324ef4f26ecaeaefe6cf5f22524f0be4c03251`, is artistically accepted,
including its documented large-world performance limits. Preserve its optional
opening, creature, source material, controls, appearance, saves and settings.
Its complete executable, installation and source remain the 3.0 rollback.

## 3.1.0: accepted engineering consolidation

Improve preservation, asset recovery, native provenance, persistence integrity,
reproducibility and developer guidance. Revalidate previous fixes before changing
them. Keep QA output private by default, with selected compact evidence deliberate.
See [final engineering review](engineering/FINAL_REVIEW_3_1.md),
[issue register](engineering/ISSUES_3_1.md) and
[recovery instructions](LOCAL_ASSET_LAYOUT.md).

The owner accepted the final lifecycle correction, source and preservation
evidence and authorized the complete 3.1.0 release. Delivery requires matching
main/development/tag, the root executable, installation and real Start Menu
launch, plus user-data preservation. See [release delivery](engineering/RELEASE_3_1.md).
Retain complete 3.0 rollback and preserve accepted 3.1 as the next baseline.

## 3.2: performance and moderate architecture work

Both large-world responsiveness and clearer subsystem ownership are approved
future objectives. [The technical plan](engineering/ARCHITECTURE_3_2.md) defines
stages, measurements, equivalence checks and rollback boundaries. Implementation
does not start automatically with completion of 3.1.

Accepted 3.2 delivery must update main/tag, version resources, changelog and docs,
the root EXE, installed executable and verified Start Menu launch. Preserve
accepted 3.1 as rollback; an EXE left under `.qa` is only a review build.

## 3.3.0: accepted atmospheric polish

The owner accepted the 3.2 source baseline and the 3.3 atmospheric candidate
`b52fa4b7`, including the documented large-world performance and memory limits.
3.3.0 combines both passes: layered rain, impacts on raised surfaces, a prepared
recorded rain soundscape and moving depth-aware fog. See the
[atmosphere review](engineering/ATMOSPHERE_3_3.md) for measured results.

The owner authorized publication through the existing development and main
branches, the annotated v3.3.0 tag and source-only release, followed by the local
EXE and Start Menu upgrade. Final delivery evidence belongs in the
[3.3 release record](engineering/RELEASE_3_3.md). The temporary preparation ceiling
is 8 GiB; aggregate QA must return below 5 GiB before the local upgrade.

Version 4 and the Cathedral interface overhaul are cancelled. Earlier 3.2
candidate status above records that pass's history; 3.3 supersedes its delivery.

## Outside this consolidation

No Cathedral redesign, creature replacement, Redstone Lab expansion or substantial
new features. The three local reference collections stay at their existing paths.
Retiring the historical research branch requires a separate owner decision after
its unique content and recovery have been reviewed. No history rewriting or tag
deletion is part of this work.
