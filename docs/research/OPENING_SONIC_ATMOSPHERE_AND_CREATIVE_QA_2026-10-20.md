## Sound palette and composition principles

**Never equate number of source clips with sound design.** Design identifiable roles and envelopes, use recorded detailed transients and room responses, then audition repeatedly in context. Build at least two meaningfully different candidate mixes and reject muddy, indistinct or clichéd material before committing. `python` may run extraction/edit/measurement; stock synthesized chirps, identical sine drones, a pitched default scream or a longer reverb on the existing flat mix are not satisfactory assets.

**Cathedral ascent:** independent causal layers with sample/timeline provenance: (1) low structural strain with audible midrange weight, (2) limestone grinding / shear, (3) individual stone contacts and chipping debris, (4) an irregular sequence of groaning joints/masonry under stress, (5) air volume / abyssal displacement, (6) spacious early reflections + restrained long-decay tail, (7) non-simultaneous monumental settling. All seven are sound-design *roles*, not necessarily seven constant simultaneous loops. Relate events to actual rising geometry/acceleration and pauses; let the end settle rather than just fade a loop. Offer dry-stem / wet-stem / final-in-scene comparison. Prevent sub-heavy inaudibility on common laptop speakers or headphones.

**Quiet ambience and echo:** make a coherent ambient bed at an actually audible level: air, distant structural resonance, occasional dust/rubble/stone settling, sparse distant voices or ambiguous tones only where dramaturgically justified. Differentiate exposed void from cathedral enclosure. A credible tail starts from the sound emitter; echo should not be identical, full-wet on every UI click. Use separate direct signal, early reflection(s) and diffuse late tail, responsive to camera/context when feasible through curated pre-rendered variants and bounded runtime crossfades; do not claim full physical acoustics/HRTF from stereo panning. Silence and near-silence are explicit intended phases, but 'missing ambience everywhere' is not.

**Piano:** identify the owner's original `panoir-you-have-time.ogg` and its legal use/distribution scope. Inspect whether its single container comprises multiple performances/pieces or phrases; do not guess cue points or cut a held note. Produce a cue sheet with human-auditioned phrase-safe start/end, tail and transition handling, and retain the master untouched. Export curated **derived private segments** if multiple real selections exist; use an existing playlist/shuffle-bag route with no immediate repeat, per-opening variety and appropriate fade/crossfade. Do not random-seek to arbitrary offsets in a compressed file at runtime; do not turn different excerpts into harsh jumps, chopped music or a permanently foregrounded score. Piano stops/cuts at the approved narrative trigger and never bleeds into the creature score. If a single recording has no coherent excerpt boundaries, report it and audition licensed alternatives rather than fabricating 'songs' or buying anything without approval.

**Interface / building:** a small semantic family with credible recorded or carefully resynthesized tactile transients: focus optional/rare, select/change, drawer open/close, cancel, validation failure, actual save completion/failure, undo/redo, material-specific place/break and mechanisms. Source fidelity and explicit event semantics outrank generic 'sci-fi whoosh'. Preserve `AudioRouter`; skip unchanged states, blank clicks, bulk sound spam, and failed saves that produce a success cue. Audition in combination with piano and ambience, not in isolation. Give UI and building effects independent gain only if needed through existing settings.

**Ten-voice climactic horror ensemble (Pass 2):** assemble and inspect ten distinctly sourced, licensed *human* vocal performances/takes that are audibly different, not eleven filename aliases to repeated previews. Include near/direct strained breath, mid-distance voiced scream, split-register/howl, distant responses, low vocal resonance, and a fractured/spatially dispersed chorus. Design at least ten individually authored tracks/roles, with staggered entries and an identifiable climactic overlap in the owner-reviewed proof; do not play ten full-volume voices from the first encounter second. Preserve formant/human recognizability in some layers while manipulating others: pitch/stretch/filter/reverse tails only where actually musical. Avoid stereotyped stock 'monster roar', unrelated dialogue, canned horror risers and constant shrieking. Tie contacts, injuries and architectural destruction to distinct rhythm rather than a generic overlay. Submit all voice stems, voice-only, environment-only, full mix and a headphone audition. Layer count is a contract, but **perceived legibility and terror are artistic acceptance**.

**Quality control:** check file paths, licensing, sample rate/channel count, clipping/true-peak risk, DC offset, audible frequency band (not only sub-bass), loop seams, phase cancellation and mono/downmix, repeated transitions, sound priorities, event-to-visual timing and subjective level against real background piano. Provide a sound cue sheet and stems/isolated auditions for owner review. No waveform statistic or 'N recordings' statement can substitute for listening.

Free bundle allows commercial media/game synchronization and modification without attribution, but **does not permit publishing/repackaging the raw or processed recordings as a sound library**, including in a public source asset repository. A finished game with synchronized sound is permitted. Recording source names, license URL/version and local digests is good practice. AI/ML training on these assets is prohibited. Do not conflate GDC bundle rights with paid-library or unrelated Freesound licenses. Never upload raw/edited source sound packs to GitHub; keep licensed source/work-in-progress assets local and package only in an authorized finished game. Document all third-party permission ambiguities before distribution.

## Visual atmosphere: audit, then enhance purposefully

Current opening has a handful of screen-space motes and stylized GPU layers. Native GPU and software paths must be captured side by side at matched phases to distinguish absent particles from minimalist thin implementation or low contrast. Benchmark emitted particles, world-space drifting/falling dust, faint distance haze / atmospheric perspective, restrained near-camera layer, light falloff and motion coherence. Favor **depth and placement** over adding more random dots or dense fog. Dust should change as masonry disturbs the space; the abyss can retain long quiet holds. Never conceal major architecture, target selection, creature silhouette or key contact points. Keep particles and sound phase-linked where motion motivates them. Preserve 1080p performance and fallback parity appropriate to each backend.

## Review process and ownership

Pass 1 includes: baseline comparison + regression fix, curated cathedral/ambience/piano redesign, UI/building semantic cues, UI visual design and measured builder performance/fidelity. It does NOT finalize the new creature's ten-voice score or replace the old creature model. Pass 2 covers original model, procedural motion, sculpted ten-voice horror ensemble, encounter choreography and final integration once silhouette/motion gates are accepted.

Internal creative process in both passes: study actual references; draft at least two substantially different looks or mixes; inspect actual exported/native results, not descriptions; critique at ordinary game scale; refine until an internally coherent candidate emerges; then request owner judgment at the prescribed artistic gate. Do not generate countless branches or micro-approval requests. Preserve known-good installed 2.9.8 and user source/audio data. Report SOURCE/TEST, NATIVE VISUAL, AUDIBLE, and INSTALLED checks separately. No high-stakes code/release promotion from subjective cues alone.
