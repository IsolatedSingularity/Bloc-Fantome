Outcome
Researched Monument Valley's design process, Gothic cathedral construction,
camera continuity, cinematic production, pacing and UI feedback. The sources,
interpretations and research limits are in ARCHITECTURE_RESEARCH_2.9.8.txt.

player's actual orbit/zoom and eases into its shot. Quiet inspection remains.
The existing creature, animation, audio, assets and extraction work are retained.

Tutorial

Edits still invalidate it normally. Visual QA also found that the lighting page
framed a Nether ceiling instead of the bastion. Its source landmark now has an
architectural selection, uses the existing reversible Terrain: Hidden mode,
faces the exposed vault and frames the ruin. All 325,478 world cells remain.
The final lighting-page render took 5.38-6.77 seconds across three resolutions;
maximum loading frames were 99-307 ms. This is not instantaneous loading, nor a
controlled comparative benchmark. Steady render p95 was 2.57-8.52 ms.
