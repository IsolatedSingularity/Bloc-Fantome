## Procedural motion: design a performance, not a solver demo

Author a vocabulary of unmistakably different full-body poses: compact observation, support-loading, exploratory reach, deliberate brace, pre-contortion compression, broken/unbalanced contortion, shifted recovery. Build an explicit sequence with states `observe -> shift_weight -> unload_contact -> reach -> acquire -> load -> settle`, plus an independent contortion/recovery branch; define changing support intent on each side, not a periodic oscillator. Long still holds are part of the performance.

For each support limb, preserve chain lengths and meaningful joint poles/limits. Use two-bone IK where fitting; constrained FABRIK for a genuinely longer chain only if useful. Contacts belong to real floor/cathedral surfaces and lock in world or moving-support-local space until intentionally released; no palm/foot sliding while the body translates. Move shoulder/trunk before unloading a limb; show weight transfer visibly. Swing clearance, reachable target handling, consistent bend side, no stretching, no underground collision. For climbing, support choice must align with real visible architecture. For contortion, the trunk/shoulders drive a brief, terrifying chained reconfiguration that alters the silhouette while supporting contacts still make sense; asymmetric recoil must not immediately return to the same looped idle shape. Quaternions/deterministic sampling may be useful but cannot excuse terrible posing.

**Checkpoint 2 proof:** After silhouette PASS, render a ~9 s neutral staging sequence with a simple floor and raised ledge: 0-1.5 s still; 1.5-2.3 s support transfer; 2.3-3.2 s reach/ledge acquisition; 3.2-4.6 s body follows with weight; 4.6-5.8 s a different rear contact adjusts asynchronously; 5.8-6.5 s coil; 6.5-6.7 s abrupt but continuous silhouette-changing distortion; 6.7-9 s irregular recoiling stillness. These are comparison beats, not immutable runtime choreography. Show continuous whole-body footage, contact-debug footage and silhouette from multiple useful angles at the real native 1080p renderer, including side and actual encounter distance. Do not conceal poor poses through cuts, glitches, fog, shake or tiny framing. Check deterministic seek/replay/focus-loss, segment lengths relative to scale, contact drift in projected pixels and world units, reachable/unreachable cases, finite vertices/normals, penetration and consistent 24/30/60/144 Hz sampling.

**Artistic PASS** asks: does it feel *enormous, connected, deliberate, unpredictable and anatomically disturbing* without merely flailing? Numerically satisfying IK is only technical PASS. If it looks like a skeleton on wires, stop and revise the shape/pose before adding detail. The original 400x400 reference's mass and fold language remains the benchmark even if exact original pixels are unavailable.

## Sonic horror: ten-layer human scream architecture, genuine weight and acoustic space

The owner explicitly rejects stock generic screams and notes that '11 recordings' in the previous handoff did not translate to rich **ten-multilayer screams**. Treat this as a high-value authored score with owner listening acceptance. The old `render_encounter_audio.py` iterates 11 voice source IDs into 22 scheduled events in a fixed WAV; it is **not** proof of ten independent layers audibly coalescing into the climax.

Build a curated, license-verified **minimum 10 separately authored human vocal tracks/takes** (distinct performances, not 10 pitch-shifted copies of one actor), plus sparse breaths/whispers as needed. Audition each recording and discard clichéd or weak examples; track exact performer/recording/source, license version and hashes. Use deliberate roles across register and perceived spatial distance: foreground bodily strain, throat/breath onset, restrained middle cry, distant answering wail, high strained break, low vocal resonance, fractured overlapping vowel clouds, near-whisper, staggered mid-distance cry and slow-release lingering tail. Preserve audible humanity in some layers. Process selected others with controlled formant shift, spectral shaping, nonlinear distortion or time-stretch where artistically justified. Tune *direct vs reflection vs diffuse tail* per position/shot. Begin with 1-2 buried sources; build through independent entrances into **a specifically audible ten-voice overlap at selected apex moments**; deliberate stagger and sparse recovery matter more than maximum wall of noise. No constant stack of ten, canned screamer jump cuts or stock dinosaur roar. Create voice-only ten-channel or ten-stem proof, at least two distinct ensemble compositions, and full in-scene proof; owner judges recognizable individual voices, dreadful shared gestalt, sense of location and absence of clipping/mush. Crucial impacts are synced to actual rig contact, not arbitrary wall-clock cue numbers after retiming.
