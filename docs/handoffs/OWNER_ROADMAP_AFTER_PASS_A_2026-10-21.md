## Pass A baseline and performance issue

Published report: 475 full-suite tests and 27 fallback checks passed, preserving 56 hash targets, but performance explicitly stayed HOLD. Median edited full repaint improved in selected worlds, while individual interaction frames still reached approximately 406 ms; the ocean-monument tutorial edit+render took approximately 720 ms and first rendered image over one second. The partial plane-like redraw was replaced with atomic repaint in source, but that can trade the visible sweep for blocking freezes. Hardware keyboard and precise-trackpad acceptance were not established by scripted tests.

**Owner's physical playtest overrules a generic PASS claim.**
- Large maps still lag loading, panning, zooming and interaction. In some large worlds the user **cannot place blocks at all**. Diagnose separately input ownership/hit testing, stale scene state, editing validity and rendering/latency. Reproduce actual mouse placement in the affected large scene, compare persisted cell and rendered world. Do not assert success from a sub-millisecond handler alone.
- Original Minecraft block textures still look bad when zoomed. Merely choosing nearest-neighbor scaling has not satisfied the visual target. Inspect native source texture resolution, projection sampling, mip/minification, compositing and exact output. Actual new high-resolution source art or substantive texture replacement requires a design decision; vectorizing low-resolution Minecraft bitmap textures cannot recover missing detail.
- Regressed lower-right Grow Canvas +, Shrink Canvas - and Terrain Noise buttons have lost their informative colored/3D thumbnail previews. Restore refined previews.
- REMOVE the large Shrink warning modal. Retain data safety without unwanted confirmation, preferably by making shrink non-destructive or undoable (owner to resolve any material behavior ambiguity).
- Remove redundant lower-left "Fill", "More tools" and selected-block text, and residual information appearing when HUD fades. Remove the word "Unsaved". Preserve essential commands through the top Tools menu/shortcuts and prevent hidden widget hitboxes.
- Remove Build drawer footer prose such as "Favorite / Ctrl+D", counts and instructions. Keep tasteful little category icons, but replace HORIZONTAL paging with a VERTICAL list/rail of categories or a compact category submenu, as owner chooses.
- Tint all existing UI slightly DARK BLUE/navy and lightly translucent. It must be restrained, airy enough to see game atmosphere but TIGHT in spacing. Eliminate oddly oversized modal/header space, empty title bars, long dead areas and mixed padding. Use consistent type scale, horizontal/vertical rhythm, corner radii, text layout, input hitboxes and microstates.
- Current Pygame rounded corners/text/UI graphics visibly pixelate at some sizes. Improve final native-resolution raster quality with scalable/vector-source geometry, antialiased strokes and native-density fonts and icons. Do not upscale previously rasterized whole-panel UI, misuse DPI transforms or change camera/picking geometry. Validate final packaged Windows output in multiple sizes, text scales and fullscreen.
- World is an **iOS Control Center-like grid**, not a list: centered ICON-ONLY toggle tiles, no permanent explanatory text, hover/focus tooltips, slightly translucent icons, meaningful unique per-function glyphs, and restrained in-tile animations showing actual ON/OFF behavior. Weather effects should animate semantically; reduced motion respected. One-shot commands belong to separate SMALLER, unanimated action controls.
- Tools: move triangular/diagonal icon-and-silhouette inset from LEFT edge to RIGHT edge of each row and refine. Keep independent scroll regions, add a distinct HOTKEYS heading in the lower section, make shortcut chords immediately legible and explanation text decluttered/subtle/italicized.
- Menu: the monotone oversized text-command list needs intentional information architecture and distinctive interaction design, researched from quality applications and games. Maintain every feature; do not merely recolor the existing list.
- Library: on first click, show a **free-floating compact four-category landing menu** (Worlds, Biomes, Structures, Saved Builds). Four LARGE icon buttons with ONE word underneath each, not an attached long dropdown/tabbed file-explorer view. Clicking a category opens a separately considered submenu/gallery with actual relevant preview thumbnails and explicit Open vs Place; changing view should feel like a compact surface transforming/opening/closing. Ratchet & Clank 1 store/vendor menus are a *motion/interaction inspiration*, not a source to copy. No awkward empty regions, oversized headers, permanent four-tab row or accidental load/place. The owner is open to alternatives after seeing principled references.
- The modern Segoe-based charcoal style is liked; **refine rather than reinvent**. The owner asks to research effective spacing, typography, responsiveness and menu hierarchy from Apple HIG, Microsoft Fluent, excellent game UI, polished desktop and web software before issuing a implementation brief.

Previously accepted Pass A behaviors still needed: contextual hotbar, precise trackpad scrolling, contextual tool abilities, tutorial restoration, updated snow/terrain response, full block catalog, valid save/undo and animations that do not steal input. Fix rather than regress them.

## Deferred Pass B: concrete approved brief

**Audio identity and sources.** Build a cohesive, premium CONTEMPORARY (not Minecraft) UI sound family: warm, low-pitched, bass-supported, highly intelligible and well-mastered interactions. Distinct hover, focus, click/press, open/close, toggle, select, confirmation, success/save, failure, loading, undo/cancel. Avoid generic stock beeps, repeated harps, excessive hover spam or cheap downloaded audio; compose from good recorded and synthesized components, with real envelope design and pitch/texture hierarchy. Research pro game/audio UX techniques. Minecraft-derived world placement/breaking/fire/water sounds remain IN WORLD; audit full block material sound mappings, use material families with specific exceptions rather than generic wrong effects. Preserve licensing, local master audio and provenance. Use freely accessible materials with no account/payment. Review Sonniss terms for pre-existing published isolated B4 derivatives; do not publish new prohibited standalone modified sound files or rewrite history casually.

**Shrine/cathedral/abyss.** The monument completion/rise is too weak. Create multiple physically synchronized layers: foundation strain, grinding moving stone, settling/stopping, distinct resonant contact "dong" from plates, convincing reverberation, background pressure/rumble and diffuse abyss ambience. Bass must be DEEP and enveloping but not muddy. Localize actual contacts and impacts with direct attack, positional early reflections, long diffuse cathedral late reverberation and audible motion. Account for camera/scene location and preserve quiet architecture/negative space. B4 Chamber is a provisional comparison, NOT approved final audio. Respect mixer pools, mute, user levels and existing original piano-chapter master.

**Temporary creature.** Keep the existing private Twilight Thorn rig for B only and use ONE strong authored encounter animation with held anticipation, sudden contortion and meaningful contact instead of generic repetitive motion. Do not start the original asset/design replacement within B.

**Creature vocals.** Sound should suggest unmistakably HUMAN distress trapped in something monstrous: high sharp screams/gasps, low strained voices, guttural resonance, whispers. Reuse/inspect the existing eleven sourced vocal samples and augment only if needed with verified no-login/free licensed recordings; create varied processed timbres, random/bounded pitch AND formant shifts, irregular timing, dynamic durations, layer selection, pauses/interruption and source-relative localization. Avoid fixed 44-second slow repetitive oscillator, artificial cycling, smeared low-bitrate muffling, uncontrolled ten-voice buildup or stock horror silliness. Deep lows coexist with occasional piercing clarity. Make motion and contacts drive the sound as much as an authored timeline does.

**Visual & flow.** Smaller/more numerous triangles in the dark faceted abyss sea, modestly faster undulation, no gaps and measured WGL performance cost. Restore unobtrusive TOP-RIGHT Skip throughout shrine/cathedral/encounter; clicking skips the ENTIRE rest of the opening to normal editor/tutorial, with correct audio/GPU cleanup and no saved-world corruption.

**Acceptance.** Integrated playable scene and direct listening, event synchronization, low-end speaker/headphone translation, intelligibility, cue variation, actual spatial character, clipping/headroom, normal UI/ordinary SFX channel behavior, preservation and performance. Offline audition mixes are not proof of real live audio or subjective listener acceptance.
